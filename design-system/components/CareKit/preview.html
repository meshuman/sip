Everything it needs for its first years

  • Wood oilGlass flask · 30 mlFeeds the wood and keeps carved edges from drying out.
  • Soft brushGoat hair · 20 mmReaches into the carving where a cloth cannot.
  • Care cardLokta · 125 × 175 mmThese steps in English and Nepali, with a code to the care film.
  1. WeeklyBrush the dust out of the carving with the soft brush. Work with the grain.
  2. Every 3 monthsBrush clean first. Shake the flask, put a few drops on the brush, and work a thin coat into the wood. After 15 minutes, wipe off any oil that has not soaked in. Leave it a day before handling.
  3. NeverWater, furniture polish or sprays. Do not oil painted surfaces.
