# CareKit

Shows what care equipment ships in the box with a piece, and how to use it.

- **The kit:** each item gets a drawing, a name, its size or quantity in the registry face, and one line on what it is for.
- **The steps:** ordered by how often. They always end with a "Never" row in sindur.
- **The footer:** links to the care film, plus a refill line tied to the piece's registry number, so the owner can reorder the right oil.

## Kits by material

| Material | In the box |
|---|---|
| Wood (Newar carving, sal or other woods) | Flask of wood oil, soft brush, care card |
| Metal (cast, gilded or hammered) | Soft brush, care card. No oil or polish, which strips gilding |
| Stone | Soft brush, care card |
| Thangka and Mithila (cloth and paper) | Care card only, with hanging and light guidance |

The wood row is the confirmed kit. The other rows are a starting proposal to confirm with each maker.

## Rules

- **Instructions come from the maker.** Agree on the oil, the amounts and the intervals with the workshop that made the piece. Write them down on the day, and never copy them from another craft.
- **One source of truth.** The same steps appear here on the piece page, on the printed care card in the box, and in the care section of the catalogue card, all from one record.
- **Short sentences.** Every step starts with a verb and names the tool from the kit it uses.
- **Show what is in the box.** Put the kit in the product photography as well: one flat-lay of the piece with its kit on lokta.

The oil quantity, the brush, the intervals and the reorder line in the preview are examples.
