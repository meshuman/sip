# MakingTimeline

The days of making drawn to scale, so a buyer sees where the time went, with each stage linked to its moment in the film.

- Stage widths are the share of total days, so they must add up to 100%.
- The current or highlighted stage takes `.is-now` (malachite), and the gilding stage takes `.is-gilt`.
- Below the scale, each stage gets its days in the registry face, a name, one sentence, and a "Watch · m:ss" link where it was filmed.

The stages change by craft:
- **Lost-wax:** wax model, mould, casting, chasing, gilding.
- **Thangka:** canvas, sketch, mineral colour, gold line, eye-opening.
- **Mithila:** paper, outline, filling, border.
- **Wood or stone:** blocking, carving, finishing.

Record the days on the workbench as the work happens. Never estimate them afterwards.
