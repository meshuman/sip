## Choosing makers

We work with makers whose craft we can see for ourselves. Before anything is listed, we visit the workshop and meet the people who work there. We agree in writing what each piece pays the workshop, and we ask each maker whether owners' notes may appear on their public page.

## Filming the making

Wherever we can, we film a piece from beginning to end. A statue can take five weeks, from the wax model to the last day of gilding. When a piece was finished before we met it, we film the same maker at the same work on another piece, and say so on the page.

## Registering a piece

Each piece gets its registry number on the day it joins the collection. We record its measurements and weight, photograph it from every side, and mark the number on the piece. For larger pieces we make a 3D scan, so you can turn the piece in your browser.

## Export clearance

Statues and paintings leaving Nepal must be cleared, so that genuine antiques stay in the country. Sīpa takes every piece through clearance before shipping. The documents are saved in the owner's record. [Read about export and customs](/help/export-and-customs).
