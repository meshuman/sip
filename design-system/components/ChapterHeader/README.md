# ChapterHeader

Opens each block of a piece page with the question as a label, a Nepali word, a display heading and a one-sentence answer.

- The label is the reader's question in plain words ("What the gesture means", "Where to place it").
- The Nepali word is one word for the chapter, in gilt-ink.
- The lead paragraph's first sentence answers the question outright; it is the line answer engines will quote.
- `.sp-chapter--place` turns the label and Nepali word lapis for the "where it was made" act.
