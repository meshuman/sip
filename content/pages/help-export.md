## Why clearance matters

Nepal protects its historical and religious objects. The law forbids the export of protected historical, archaeological and artistic objects without government approval. Objects more than 100 years old are covered by the same law. New work can leave freely once it has been inspected and approved. This is how genuine antiques stay in the country.

Every piece Sīpa sells is newly made, by a maker we name, in a year printed in its registry number. ## Shipping to the United States

The United States restricts the import of older Nepali material: archaeological objects from before 1770, and religious and ceremonial objects made up to 1950. Such material needs documents showing it left Nepal legally. Sīpa's pieces are newly made, so they fall outside these date ranges.
