## What is a registry number?

It is a number given to every Sīpa piece for life, such as PTN-MT-26-0142. It shows where the piece was made, by which craft, in which year, and which piece it is. [Check a number](/registry).

## Is every piece one of a kind?

Yes. Every piece is made by hand and recorded on its own. Two statues of the same figure by the same maker still differ, and each gets its own number and record.

## Are the makers paid fairly?

Artisans are paid a fair share of every sale, enough to sustain their livelihood. The share is agreed with each maker before a piece is listed.

## Can I write to the maker?

Yes. Once your piece is recorded to you, you can write a note from your owner's record. We translate it into Nepali and read it to the maker at the workshop. Some makers reply.

## Will my name be shown?

Only if you choose. Your piece's public record says "a private owner" unless you agree to show your first name and city. Notes to makers are private unless you tick the box to show them on the maker's page.

## What comes in the box?

The piece, its catalogue card printed on lokta paper, and a care kit for its material. A wooden piece, for example, comes with a flask of wood oil and a soft brush.

## Is the film showing my exact piece?

Where we could, yes: we film a piece from its first step to its last. When a piece was finished before we met it, the film shows the same maker at the same work, and the page says so.

## Can I see a piece from every side?

Larger pieces have a 3D view you can turn in your browser, with labels on their parts. Every piece has photographs from several sides, with one showing its scale.

## Can I send a piece as a gift?

Yes. Choose gift at checkout, add a message for the card, and leave the price off the packing slip. You can pass the record to the person you gave it to later.

## Is it legal to take these pieces out of Nepal?

Yes, once they are cleared. Every Sīpa piece is newly made and cleared for export before it ships, and the documents travel with it. [Read about export and customs](/help/export-and-customs).

## Can I visit the makers?

Some makers welcome visitors to their workshops. Visits open after launch; [write to us](/contact) in the meantime and we'll try to arrange one.

## What if my piece arrives damaged?

Photograph everything and write to us within 7 days. We'll arrange a repair, a replacement or a full refund. [Read about returns](/help/returns).
