# RegistrySeal

The registry number with its gilt halo, the brand's one circle and its mark of provenance.

- **Unrecorded** (outline ring): on the piece page before purchase, with the registration date.
- **Owned** (`.sp-seal--owned`, filled ring): in the owner's record and on the sold piece page. Show the owner's name only as they chose (initial and city by default).
- **Drawing** (`.sp-seal--drawing`): the one orchestrated animation, played once when a piece is recorded to its owner.

The letter in the ring is स, the first letter of सीप. Never shorten the number, change its colour, or use the halo for anything that is not a record.
