## Currencies

Prices are set in Nepali rupees. You can view them in US dollars or euros at today's rate, and you are charged in the currency shown at checkout. Your bank may add its own conversion fee.

## When you're charged

You are charged when you place the order. If a piece can't be cleared for export or shipped, we refund you in full within 5 working days.
