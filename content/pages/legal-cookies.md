# Cookies

Sīpa uses a small number of cookies. None of them are for advertising.

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Bag | Keeps the pieces in your bag | 30 days |
| Session | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out, or 30 days |
| Preferences | Remembers your currency and language | 1 year |
| Analytics | Counts visits to pages, without identifying you ({{TODO: tool}}) | {{TODO}} |

You can choose "Only essential" in the cookie banner, or change your mind at any time in your browser. Essential cookies (bag, session and preferences) are needed for the site to work.
