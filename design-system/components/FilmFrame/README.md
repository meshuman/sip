# FilmFrame

The making film in a 16:9 frame, with a paper play button and chapter marks that match the MakingTimeline stages.

- The poster frame is a still from the film, never a title card. Do not put text over the poster except the play button.
- Chapters use the same names as the timeline, with start times in the registry face.
- The credit line says where and over how long it was filmed, and where the full record lives.
- Cut every hero piece to three lengths: 20 s (social and the share card), about 2 min (this frame), and the full record (the maker's page).
- Every film needs captions in English and Nepali.
