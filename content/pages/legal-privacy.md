# Privacy

Last updated {{TODO: date}}

This page explains what Sīpa collects about you, why, and what you control. The short version: we collect what we need to sell, ship and record your piece. We show nothing about you publicly unless you agree. We never sell your information.

## What we collect

- **To sell and ship:** your name, email, phone, delivery address and order details. Payments are handled by {{TODO: provider}}, and we never see your card number.
- **For the registry:** which pieces are recorded to you, and when.
- **For notes to makers:** what you write and whether you allow it to be shown.
- **To run the site:** basic usage information and cookies. See [Cookies](/legal/cookies).

## What is public

A piece's public record shows "a private owner" unless you allow your first name and city to be shown. You can change this at any time in [Settings](/account/settings). Notes to makers are shared with the maker and our team. They appear on the maker's page only if you tick the box.

## Who sees your information

- **Our team:** to fulfil your order.
- **Our shipping carrier and customs agent:** to deliver your piece and clear it.
- **Our payment provider:** to take payment.
- **The maker:** only the notes you send, and your first name and city.

## How long we keep it

We keep order and registry information for as long as the piece's record exists, because the record is part of the piece. You can ask us to remove your name from a record at any time; it will then say "a private owner". {{TODO: retention periods for other data}}
