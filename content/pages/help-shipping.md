## Packing and insurance

Every piece is packed to travel: wrapped, braced inside its box, and double-boxed. {{TODO: materials, e.g. recycled paper fill, wooden crate for stone over 10 kg}}. Every shipment is insured for the full price you paid until it is delivered.

## Gifts

You can send a piece as a gift at checkout. Add a message for the card, and choose to leave the price off the packing slip. The piece is recorded to you. You can pass the record to the person you gave it to later.

## Delivery problems

If your piece arrives damaged, keep the box and packing and photograph everything before you unpack further. Then [write to us](/contact) within 7 days. See [Returns](/help/returns) for what happens next.
