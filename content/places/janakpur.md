## The place

Janakpur lies on the plains of the Madhesh, close to the border with India. Tradition holds that it was the city of King Janaka and the birthplace of his daughter Sita, and its white Janaki Mandir temple draws pilgrims from across Nepal and India, especially at the festival of Vivah Panchami, which celebrates Sita's wedding to Rama.

## The craft

Mithila painting began on the mud walls of homes in this region, painted by women for weddings and festivals. Janakpur has become its centre in Nepal, where painters work on paper and cloth. [Read about Mithila painting](/crafts/mithila-painting).
