# ShareCard

The 1200 × 630 image a link to a piece shows when it is shared on WhatsApp, Viber, Facebook, LinkedIn or email.

Render one per piece from the same record, as the page's `og:image`. Left: the hero plate, cropped to the figure. Right: the brand, the piece name, maker and place with days, and the registry seal.

Variants, all on the same layout:
- **Piece:** "Made by Raju Shakya in Patan over 38 days."
- **Recorded:** after purchase, for the owner to share. Use the filled seal and the line "Now kept in Zürich. Recorded 15 Oct 2026."
- **Maker:** the maker's portrait in the plate, their name as the title, and their place and craft beneath.

Keep every word to one line where possible. Never put a price on a share card. A default card for the site is in the Share asset group.
