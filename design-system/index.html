Colour

  • lokta#ecebe5 · #161917

    Page ground. Named for Nepal's handmade lokta paper: a cool, fibrous grey-white, never cream. In dark, the near-black of oiled sal wood.

  • lokta-deep#e1dfd7 · #1e2220

    Recessed bands: the story chapters, the footer, the 3D viewer stage.

  • paper#f7f6f2 · #232825

    Raised surfaces that stand for a physical document: the object label, the catalogue card, the registry record.

  • line#c9c6bb · #3a413d

    Hairlines, 1px. Dividers between label rows and input borders. Borders, not shadows.

  • ink#1c1c19 · #ece8df

    Primary text on lokta, lokta-deep and paper.

  • stone#5d5c55 · #a9a69c

    Secondary text and metadata on any ground. Named for Patan's grey basalt.

  • malachite#1f5547 · #79b5a1

    The one action colour: primary buttons, links, the active chapter, focus rings. The green of ground malachite in thangka painting.

  • malachite-wash#d5e3dc · #1b3029

    Tinted ground behind a selected option or the impact panel. Text on it is ink or malachite.

  • on-malachite#f7f6f2 · #0f1f1a

    Text and icons on a malachite fill.

  • gilt#987431 · #d4b06a

    Fire-gilding gold. Reserved for provenance: the registry seal, the halo rule, the ownership mark. Never a button, never body text on lokta in light (use gilt-ink).

  • gilt-ink#6e521c · #d4b06a

    Gilt as readable text: registry numbers and the 'Recorded' line on lokta or paper.

  • lapis#2a3e68 · #93a9d8

    Place and journey: the map pin, region names, the 'where it was made' chapter. Ground lapis from thangka blues.

  • sindur#9e2b20 · #e0897c

    Signal only: 'one of one', 'last piece', errors. Small, never a block.
