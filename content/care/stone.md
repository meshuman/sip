## Where to keep it

Stone is strong but porous. It soaks up oil, water and smoke, and the marks rarely come out. In the Kathmandu Valley, stone figures in shrines darken with years of offerings, and many people value that. If you would rather keep yours clean, keep oil lamps, incense and wet offerings away from it.

## Outdoors

Stone figures can stand in a garden or courtyard. In places where it freezes, bring the figure indoors for winter or raise it off the ground and cover it. Water that freezes inside stone can crack it.

## Weight

Stone is heavy for its size. Place it on a surface rated for its weight, which is shown on its page and its card, and never on a shelf edge.

## If something goes wrong

A chip or crack can usually be repaired by the carver. [Write to us](/contact) with a photo and the registry number.
