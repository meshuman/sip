# Hotspot

Numbered labels pinned to a 3D model or a still, with the same labels repeated as a list beneath the stage.

- Use three to seven hotspots per piece. Each names a part of the piece (iconography or technique) with one line about it.
- Where the part was filmed, the line gives the days of making in the registry face and links to that moment in the film.
- Dots are paper with an ink ring. The open dot turns malachite, and its card opens to the right, or to the left with `.sp-hot--left` near the stage's right edge.
- On phones the floating cards are hidden. The list below the stage (`.sp-hotlist`) always carries every label, which is also the text search and answer engines read.
- Position each hotspot in percent of the stage, so it holds at every width.
- The stage is lokta-deep, 16:10, with a small "Drag to turn" hint in the registry face.
