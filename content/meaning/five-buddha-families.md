## What they show

In the Buddhism practised in the Kathmandu Valley and across the Himalaya, five Buddhas are often shown together. Each one presides over a direction and turns a confusion into a form of wisdom. You can see them in the niches of stupas in the Valley, including Swayambhunath, each facing its direction.

| Buddha | Direction | Colour | Gesture | Symbol |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vairochana | Centre | White | Teaching | Wheel |
| Akshobhya | East | Blue | Earth-touching | Vajra |
| Ratnasambhava | South | Yellow | Giving | Jewel |
| Amitabha | West | Red | Meditation | Lotus |
| Amoghasiddhi | North | Green | Fearlessness | Double vajra |

## How to recognise them

Unlike Shakyamuni, the five Buddhas are often shown crowned and jewelled. Read the gesture first, then the colour if the figure is painted, then the symbol on the base or in the hand. In metal, the gesture alone usually tells you which Buddha it is.
