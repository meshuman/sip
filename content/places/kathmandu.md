## The place

Kathmandu grew around its own Durbar Square, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and spread across the Valley floor. Two of its great Buddhist sites, Boudhanath and Swayambhunath, are also part of the Kathmandu Valley World Heritage Site.

## The crafts

The lanes around Boudhanath hold dozens of thangka studios, where students learn from masters and paintings take months to finish. The old city also keeps metal and woodcarving workshops in its Newar neighbourhoods.

## Walking the workshops

Many thangka studios around Boudhanath welcome visitors to watch painters at work. Some of Sīpa's makers welcome visitors; see [Visit a workshop](/visit).
