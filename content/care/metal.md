## Where to keep it

Metal is the most forgiving material in the collection. Keep it out of damp rooms and away from the steam of a kitchen. If it stands on an altar, put butter lamps and incense to one side, so their smoke doesn't settle on the gilding.

## Gilding

Fire-gilding is bonded to the metal and lasts for generations, but it is thin. Handle gilded areas as little as you can: fingertips leave oils that dull it over time. If you need to move a gilded statue often, wear clean cotton gloves.

## Patina

Copper and bronze that are not gilded darken slowly. This patina protects the metal and is part of its character. Don't polish it away.

## Offerings

If you make offerings of water or flowers, place them in a dish in front of the figure, not on it. Water left on metal marks it.

## If something goes wrong

Green spots on bare metal can be a sign of damp. Dry the piece, move it somewhere drier, and [write to us](/contact) with a photo and the registry number.
