## Where to keep it

Keep carved wood out of direct sun, which fades and dries it, and away from radiators, fireplaces and air-conditioning vents. Wood moves with the air around it. A carving that went from Nepal's monsoon to a heated room in winter needs a few weeks to settle, so oil it once in its first month.

## Cleaning

Use the soft brush, not a cloth, which catches on carved edges and leaves fibres in the cuts. Never use water or household cleaners.

## Oiling

The oil in your kit is the one your carver uses. A thin coat every three months is enough in most homes; in a very dry or heated room, oil it every two months. Too much oil leaves a sticky surface that holds dust. Always wipe off what hasn't soaked in after 15 minutes. Oily cloths can heat up as they dry, so lay them flat outdoors before throwing them away.

## If something goes wrong

- **A fine crack:** small surface checks can open in a dry season and often close again. Oil the area and keep the carving away from heat. If a crack keeps growing, [write to us](/contact) with a photo and the registry number.
- **Fine powder under the carving:** this can be a sign of woodworm. Move the piece away from other wood and write to us at once.
