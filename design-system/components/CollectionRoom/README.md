# CollectionRoom

A collection hung like a gallery room. A room header sits on an ink rule, then a wall where the lead piece hangs large and the rest at regular size.

- **Rooms are curatorial, not filters.** Group pieces by craft and place ("The lost-wax room · Metal from Patan"), by maker, or by a story ("For a new home"). The room line gives its number, name and piece count.
- **The intro is one or two sentences** about the place and the craft. It is also the room page's answer-engine summary.
- **The lead piece** (first card) spans two rows on desktop and the full width on phones. Choose it by story, not by price.
- **Walls are at most three columns.** Use `.sp-gallery` for plain grids such as search results.
