## Where to hang it

Choose an inside wall, away from windows that get direct sun. Mineral colours are stable, but the cotton, the silk mount and some colours fade in strong light. Keep it away from kitchen steam, incense smoke and butter lamps, which settle on the surface and darken it.

## The silk cover

Most thangkas come with a thin silk cover that hangs over the painting. Many owners keep it down and lift it only when they look at the painting or practise. It is the simplest protection there is.

## Storing and moving

Never fold a thangka: folds crack the paint. Roll it loosely from the bottom, around its lower rod, with the cover in place, and wrap it in clean cotton rather than plastic, which traps damp.

## If something goes wrong

Don't try to clean or touch up the painted surface. If you see flaking, mould or insect damage, roll it carefully as above and [write to us](/contact) with photos and the registry number.
