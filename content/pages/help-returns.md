## If it arrives damaged

Photograph the box, the packing and the piece before you unpack further, and [write to us](/contact) within 7 days of delivery. Include the registry number. We'll arrange a repair by the maker, a replacement where one exists, or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You won't pay for return shipping on a damaged piece.

## If it isn't right for you

You can return a piece within 14 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packing, with its catalogue card and unopened care kit. Write to us first and we'll send return instructions. Return shipping and any import duties paid are not refundable. We refund the price you paid within 10 working days of receiving the piece back.

## What happens to the record

When a piece comes back, its record notes the return. The piece then goes back on sale with its full history. Your name never appears on a returned piece's public record.

## Pieces made to order

Commissions and pieces made to your size or request can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or differ from what we agreed in writing.
