How to care for it · Gilded copper

  1. WeeklyDust with a soft, dry brush. Work into the folds of the robe.
  2. YearlyWipe with a dry cotton cloth. Check that the base seal is intact.
  3. MovingLift from the base with both hands, never by the head or hands.
  4. NeverWater, metal polish or cleaning sprays. They strip the gilding.
