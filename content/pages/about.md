## The piece

Every piece is explained before it is sold. Its page says what it depicts, what that means, who it suits, where it is customarily placed, and how to look after it. The same facts are printed on a card that travels in the box. Its material and tools come in a care kit made for it.

## The maker

Every piece is made by a named person in a named place. We film makers at work, ideally across the whole making of the piece you buy. Makers are paid a fair share of every sale, enough to sustain their livelihood. Owners can write to the maker, and we read every note aloud at the workshop.

## The record

Every piece gets a registry number when it joins the collection. The number is marked on the piece, printed on its card, and kept in a record that lists each owner from then on. The record is how a gift, an inheritance or a resale keeps its story.
