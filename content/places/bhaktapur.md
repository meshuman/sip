## The place

Bhaktapur keeps more of its old fabric than any other city in the Valley: brick lanes, open squares, temples and courtyards. Its Durbar Square is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the city is known for its pottery, its festivals and its carved wood.

## The crafts

Bhaktapur's woodcarvers made the struts, doorways and windows that define the Valley's architecture. The best-known example is the Peacock Window, a latticed window carved around a spreading peacock. Carvers here still restore old buildings and make new figures and panels. Bhaktapur is also home to schools and studios of thangka painting.

## Walking the workshops

Carving workshops open onto the lanes around Tachupal and Dattatreya Square, where you can watch the work from the doorway. Some of Sīpa's makers welcome visitors; see [Visit a workshop](/visit).
