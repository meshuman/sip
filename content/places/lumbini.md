## The place

Lumbini is where Queen Maya Devi gave birth to Siddhartha Gautama, who would become the Buddha. The Maya Devi Temple marks the spot. Beside it stands a stone pillar raised by the Indian emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE, whose inscription records his visit to the Buddha's birthplace. The sacred garden and the monastic zone around it, with monasteries built by Buddhist countries from across the world, make Lumbini a place of pilgrimage. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.

## From the land of the Buddha's birth

Sīpa's Buddha figures come from makers across Nepal, the country of the Buddha's birth. A piece is described as "Made in Lumbini" only when its maker works here.
