## Framing

Natural colours sit on the surface of the paper and can rub off. A framer should use a mount (a card window) so that the painting doesn't touch the glass, and acid-free materials behind it. UV-filtering glass slows fading and is worth the cost for a painting you will live with for years.

## Where to hang it

Choose a wall out of direct sun, away from bathrooms, kitchens and outside walls that get damp. Handmade paper moves with humidity. A painting that ripples a little in a wet season will usually settle again.

## Storing

Keep unframed paintings flat, between sheets of acid-free paper, in a folder or a drawer. Don't stack heavy things on top.

## If something goes wrong

Spots of mould, or colour lifting from the paper, need a paper conservator. [Write to us](/contact) with photos and the registry number, and we'll advise.
